# Citation styles

Use the style set in the thesis brief. Default: **OSCOLA** (law), **APA 7** (other fields), **Harvard** on request. If the university has its own variant (e.g. a Harvard flavour), follow the university's guide where it differs and note the differences in the brief.

**Golden rule:** format only details you actually have from the source itself (title page, imprint page, metadata, official database page). Missing detail → `[year?]`, `[publisher?]`, `[PINPOINT NEEDED]`. Never fill gaps from memory.

> Examples marked *(illustrative)* use invented names to show the format only - never cite them. Examples without that mark are real primary sources used for format.

---

## 1. OSCOLA (4th edition) - default

### General rules
- Citations go in **footnotes** (in drafts: markdown footnotes `[^1]`). The footnote marker goes after punctuation, at the end of the clause or sentence it supports.
- Every footnote ends with a full stop.
- **Minimal punctuation:** no full stops in abbreviations (`edn`, `ch`, `para`, `art`, `s`, `pt`), no comma before a pinpoint unless the citation ends with brackets around a non-date element.
- **Pinpoints:** page numbers bare (`45`), paragraphs in square brackets for cases (`[23]`), sections/articles abbreviated (`s 19`, `art 92`, `reg 4`, `para 3.2`).
- Italicise case names and titles of books/reports; article titles go in single quotation marks, not italics.
- **Subsequent citations:** short form + cross-reference: `Smith (n 3) 45.` For cases, a short name: `Donoghue (n 2) 580.`
- **Ibid** for the immediately preceding footnote: `ibid 46.` (lower case unless starting the footnote: `Ibid.`)
- Avoid *op cit*, *loc cit*, *supra*, *infra* - use `n` cross-references.
- **Bibliography:** separate lists for (1) cases, (2) legislation, (3) secondary sources (books, articles, reports, websites) - many universities also split primary/secondary. Secondary sources in the bibliography: **surname first, then initials**, no pinpoints, no full stop at the end. Cases and legislation are often listed in a Table of Cases / Table of Legislation instead.

### UK legislation
- Footnote: `Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, s 19.`
- Amended Acts: `Financial Services and Markets Act 2023.`
- Statutory instruments: `Name of SI Year, SI Year/Number, reg X.` *(fill number only from the SI itself)*

### EU legislation
- Regulation: `Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on prudential requirements for credit institutions and investment firms and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 [2013] OJ L176/1, art 92.`
- Directive: `Directive 2011/61/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 June 2011 on Alternative Investment Fund Managers and amending Directives 2003/41/EC and 2009/65/EC and Regulations (EC) No 1060/2009 and (EU) No 1095/2010 [2011] OJ L174/1.` (Use the full title exactly as in the OJ; a short name such as "CRR" or "AIFMD" can be defined in the first footnote: `... (CRR)`.)
- After onshoring, UK versions of EU law: cite as retained/assimilated law, e.g. `Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 ... as it forms part of domestic law by virtue of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018.` Check the university's preference.

### Cases
- UK: `Donoghue v Stevenson [1932] AC 562 (HL) 580.` - name *in italics*, year in [square brackets] when the year identifies the volume, report, first page, (court) where not obvious, pinpoint.
- Neutral citations first where available: `Case Name [Year] UKSC 12, [Year] 1 WLR 345 [23].` *(illustrative structure)*
- CJEU: `Case C-270/12 United Kingdom v Parliament and Council EU:C:2014:18, para 45.` (case number, name in italics, ECLI-style identifier; official report if available).
- Do not give a case citation unless the case appears in the library or was verified online.

### Supervisory and official documents
- `Author/Organisation, *Title* (Series/Number if any, Publisher/Organisation Year) pinpoint.`
- `Bank of England, *Financial Stability Report* (Month Year) 23.` *(fill month/year from the document)*
- `Financial Stability Board, *Title of Report* (FSB Year) 12.`
- `European Systemic Risk Board, *Title* (ESRB Year) 7.`
- Consultation papers: `Prudential Regulation Authority, *Title* (CP12/34, Year) para 2.3.` *(illustrative numbers)*

### Books and chapters
- Book: `Jane Smith, *Regulating Shadow Banking* (2nd edn, OUP 2024) 45.` *(illustrative)*
- Chapter: `Jane Smith, 'Title of Chapter' in Alex Brown (ed), *Title of Book* (Publisher Year) 67.` *(illustrative)*
- Bibliography form: `Smith J, *Regulating Shadow Banking* (2nd edn, OUP 2024)` *(illustrative)*

### Journal articles
- `Jane Smith, 'Title of Article' (2023) 12 Journal of Financial Regulation 45, 50.` *(illustrative)* - year in round brackets when the volume number is given; journal name in full or OSCOLA abbreviation, not italicised; first page, then pinpoint.
- Online-only / forthcoming: add `<DOI or URL> accessed 1 January 2026` only if the university requires it.
- Bibliography form: `Smith J, 'Title of Article' (2023) 12 Journal of Financial Regulation 45` *(illustrative)*

### Working papers, speeches, news, websites
- Working paper: `Jane Smith, 'Title' (Series Working Paper No 123, Year) 8.` *(illustrative)*
- Speech: `Speaker, 'Title' (Speech at Event, Place, Day Month Year) 3.` *(fill from the source)*
- News: `Author, 'Headline' *Newspaper* (Place, Day Month Year) page.`
- Website: `Author/Organisation, 'Title' (Website name, Day Month Year) <URL> accessed Day Month Year.`

## 2. APA 7th edition

### General rules
- In-text author-date: `(Smith, 2023, p. 45)` or `Smith (2023) argued ... (p. 45)`. Two authors: `(Smith & Brown, 2023)`. Three or more: `(Smith et al., 2023)`. Organisations: `(Financial Stability Board [FSB], 2023)` first time, then `(FSB, 2023)`.
- Reference list alphabetical by first author's surname, hanging indent, double-spaced.
- Sentence case for article/book titles; title case and italics for journal names; italics for book and report titles.
- Include DOIs as `https://doi.org/...` when available.

### Formats *(illustrative)*
- Journal article: `Smith, J., & Brown, A. (2023). Liquidity mismatch in open-ended funds. *Journal of Financial Stability*, *64*(2), 101-120. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx`
- Book: `Smith, J. (2024). *Regulating shadow banking* (2nd ed.). Oxford University Press.`
- Chapter: `Smith, J. (2024). Title of chapter. In A. Brown (Ed.), *Title of book* (pp. 67-89). Publisher.`
- Report by organisation: `Financial Stability Board. (Year). *Title of report*. URL`
- Legislation (APA uses legal style for laws): `Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, c. 8 (UK).` - many UK universities prefer OSCOLA-style legal citations even in APA papers; check.
- Webpage: `Author/Organisation. (Year, Month Day). *Title*. Site Name. URL`

## 3. Harvard (generic "author-date" Harvard - universities vary)

### General rules
- In-text: `(Smith 2023, p. 45)` or `Smith (2023, p. 45)`. Two authors: `(Smith and Brown 2023)`. Three or more: `(Smith et al. 2023)`.
- Reference list alphabetical, titles of books/reports/journals in italics, article titles in single quotation marks.
- Online sources: `Available at: URL (Accessed: Day Month Year).`

### Formats *(illustrative)*
- Journal article: `Smith, J. and Brown, A. (2023) 'Liquidity mismatch in open-ended funds', *Journal of Financial Stability*, 64(2), pp. 101-120. doi: 10.xxxx/xxxxx.`
- Book: `Smith, J. (2024) *Regulating shadow banking*. 2nd edn. Oxford: Oxford University Press.`
- Chapter: `Smith, J. (2024) 'Title of chapter', in Brown, A. (ed.) *Title of book*. Place: Publisher, pp. 67-89.`
- Report: `Financial Stability Board (Year) *Title of report*. Basel: FSB. Available at: URL (Accessed: Day Month Year).`
- Legislation: `Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, c. 8. Available at: URL (Accessed: ...).`

## Markers for gaps
- `[CITATION NEEDED: kind of source]` - a claim needs a source not in the library. Be specific about the kind: "CJEU case on ESMA powers", "Bank of England data on LDI leverage", "academic article on step-in risk".
- `[PINPOINT NEEDED]` - the source is known (cited) but the page/paragraph isn't confirmed.
- These markers are counted by the library page; don't use other bracket formats for gaps.
